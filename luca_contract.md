## 4. REVENUE SHARING

### 4.1. Eligible Revenue

The Creator shall be entitled to compensation **only** from revenue directly attributable to customers acquired through the Creator's content and referral efforts ("Creator-Attributed Revenue"). This includes users who:

- Downloaded the App via a unique referral link, promo code, or trackable URL assigned to the Creator; or
- Can be reasonably attributed to the Creator's content through platform analytics (e.g., TikTok attribution, App Store campaign tracking, or any mutually agreed-upon tracking mechanism).

### 4.2. Non-Eligible Revenue

The Creator shall have **no claim whatsoever** to revenue generated from:

- Organic downloads not attributable to the Creator;
- Paid advertising campaigns run by the App Owners;
- Partnerships, collaborations, or promotions by other creators or influencers;
- Press coverage, media features, or word-of-mouth referrals;
- Any other acquisition channel not directly tied to the Creator's tracked efforts;
- Revenue from users who were initially acquired through non-Creator channels, even if they later interact with the Creator's content.

### 4.3. Profit Calculation

For the purposes of this Agreement, **"Net Profit"** from Creator-Attributed Revenue shall be calculated as follows:

**Gross Revenue** (total revenue from Creator-attributed users)
*Less* the following deductions:

| Deduction | Description |
|---|---|
| Apple App Store Commission | Currently ~15–30% of gross revenue (as per Apple's policies) |
| Payment Processing Fees | RevenueCat, Stripe, or other payment/subscription management services |
| Taxes & Statutory Deductions | Applicable withholding taxes, GST (India), or sales tax |

**Net Profit = Gross Revenue from Creator-Attributed Users − All Above Deductions**

### 4.4. Creator's Share

The Creator shall receive **fifty percent (50%)** of the Net Profit as defined in Section 4.3, calculated on a monthly basis.

### 4.5. Payment Terms

- Payments shall be calculated on a **calendar month** basis.
- The App Owners shall provide the Creator with a detailed revenue report within **fifteen (15) days** of each month's end.
- Payment shall be remitted within **thirty (30) days** of each month's end.
- Payments shall be made via **[wire transfer / PayPal / Wise / other]** to the Creator's designated account.
- All payments shall be made in **US Dollars (USD)**.
- The Creator is solely responsible for reporting and paying all applicable taxes in their jurisdiction (United States).

### 4.6. Revenue Dashboard

The App Owners shall provide the Creator with access to a dedicated revenue dashboard where the Creator can view, in real time or near-real time:

- Total Creator-Attributed Revenue (gross and net);
- Number of Creator-attributed users and active subscribers;
- Breakdown of deductions (platform commissions, processing fees, etc.);
- Monthly and cumulative earnings;
- Payment history and upcoming payment estimates.

The App Owners shall ensure the dashboard is kept accurate, up to date, and accessible to the Creator at all times during the term of this Agreement and for a period of **ninety (90) days** following termination.

### 4.7. Audit Rights

Either party may request an audit of revenue records with **thirty (30) days' written notice**, no more than once per calendar year. The audited party shall provide reasonable access to relevant financial records, analytics dashboards, and third-party platform reports.

## 5. CONTENT OBLIGATIONS

### 5.1. Content Volume

- The Creator shall produce approximately **one (1) video per day**, or a minimum of **twenty-five (25) videos per calendar month**.
- Exceptions for illness, emergency, or mutually agreed breaks shall be communicated in advance where possible.

### 5.2. Content Posting

The Creator agrees to post content on the following channels:

- **Dedicated App TikTok Account** (jointly managed): All produced content shall be posted here.
- **Creator's Personal TikTok Account**: The Creator may, at their discretion, cross-post select content to their personal account to amplify reach. The App Owners acknowledge this is voluntary and appreciate the additional exposure.

### 5.3. Content Standards

- All content must be truthful, non-misleading, and compliant with TikTok's Community Guidelines and advertising policies.
- Content must comply with FTC disclosure requirements (U.S.) — all posts must include clear and conspicuous disclosure that the Creator has a financial relationship with the App (e.g., #ad, #sponsored, or "paid partnership" labels).
- The App Owners reserve the right to request reasonable revisions or removal of content that is factually inaccurate, damaging to the brand, or violates platform terms.

### 5.4. Content Ownership

- Content posted on the **Dedicated App TikTok Account** shall be jointly owned, with the App Owners retaining full rights to use, repurpose, and redistribute such content.
- Content posted on the **Creator's Personal Account** shall remain the Creator's intellectual property, with the App Owners granted a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use such content for marketing purposes.
