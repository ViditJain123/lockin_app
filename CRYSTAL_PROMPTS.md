# Crystal Generation Prompts — Lockin XP Rewards

Copy-paste prompts for all 30 crystal medals. Each prompt is **fully self-contained** — paste one block into your AI image generator and you're done. Style anchor is inlined into every prompt.

**Output spec for all 30:** 1024×1024 PNG, pure flat white background `#FFFFFF`, single centered gemstone, soft drop shadow beneath. After generation, run background removal and save to `assets/images/crystals/<tier>/<filename>.png`.

> If your background remover struggles on the lighter stones (Quartz, Moonstone, Diamond) because they share white tones with the bg, regenerate those with `pure flat white background (#FFFFFF)` swapped to `pure flat 50% gray background (#808080)` — easier alpha extraction, same visual result.
